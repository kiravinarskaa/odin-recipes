Eggplant Soup Recipe


Eggplant soup is a warm and flavorful dish made with soft eggplant, vegetables, meat, and spices. It has a rich tomato-based broth and is a comforting meal that is simple and filling.

Ingredients

  • Eggplants
  • Ground beef (or chicken/turkey)
  • Onoin
  • Bell pepper
  • Crushed tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Water or stock
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cumin
  • Smoked paprika
  • Dried mint
  • Cooked pasta (optionalFlour)

Cooking steps

  1. Chop the onion, garlic, eggplants, and bell pepper.
  2. Heat oil in a pot over medium heat.
  3. Cook onions until soft.
  4. Add garlic, meat, and bell pepper. Cook for a few minutes.
  5. Add eggplant and tomatoes.
  6. Pour in water or stock.
  7. Add salt, pepper, cumin, and paprika.
  8. Simmer until vegetables become soft.
  9. Add cooked pasta if desired.
  10. Sprinkle mint on top and serve hot.
