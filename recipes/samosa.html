Samosa


Uzbek samosa is a traditional baked pastry filled with meat, onions, and spices. It is popular in Uzbekistan and is often cooked in a tandoor oven or regular oven. Samosa has a crispy outer layer and a juicy, flavorful filling inside.

Ingredients

For the Dough

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Vegetable oil or butter

For the Filling

  • Ground lamb or beef
  • Onion
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cumin
  • Vegetable oil or animal fat

Cooking steps

  1. Prepare the dough by mixing flour, water, salt, and oil.
  2. Knead the dough until soft and smooth.
  3. Let the dough rest for from 15 to 20 minutes.
  4. Cut onions into small pieces.
  5. Mix meat, onion, salt, pepper, and cumin in a bowl.
  6. Roll out the dough into thin circles or squares.
  7. Put the meat filling in the center of each dough piece.
  8. Fold and shape the samosa into triangles or squares.
  9. Seal the edges well.
  10. Place the samosa on a baking tray.
  11. Bake in the oven until golden brown and crispy.
  12. Serve hot.
